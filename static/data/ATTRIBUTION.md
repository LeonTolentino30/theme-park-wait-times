# Attraction geographic data

`attractions.geojson` is an OpenStreetMap-derived geographic extract, available under ODbL 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Keep this attribution/licence and the per-feature source URLs when redistributing the dataset. This data licence does not change the application's source-code licence.

Coordinates were verified on November 10, 2026 through the documented Overpass API, using named attraction nodes and bounding-box centres of mapped attraction buildings/stations. They are approximate attraction locations, not surveyed entrances or routing instructions. Each feature records the original OSM object type/ID, source name/URL and coordinate method. No wait times or private provider responses are included. Attraction names and trademarks belong to their owners; no endorsement is implied.
